# Agent workflow

This repo is a phone-first crowd shooter, 柴犬軍団. One stage is the product. Do not add rules.

## Ship when it is ready

- After a focused play or feel change, run `npm test` and a phone-width check (390×844).
- If CI is green and the change does not regress sliding, gates, drums, the boss rocket, or the one-tap retry, open a PR. Do not merge unless the task says to.
- Prefer one concern per PR.

## Keep the sheet

- Tunable numbers live in `src/game/config.ts`. Stage 1 stays on that sheet. Stage 2 adds sliding gates, stage 3 adds shield cats, stage 4 adds a second boss, and stage 5 onward stacks those and raises horde HP by 0.15 per stage. Each new element appears once in a safe form during the first 10 seconds, then for real later. Boss HP is a per-stage multiplier on the stage 1 value, never the live troop size: stage 2 is 1.36×, stage 3 is 1.53×, stage 4 is 1.16× on each of two bosses. Stages 5–8 were back-solved so a skilled run has about 2.4 seconds left: 1.029× on each boss, split evenly, including stage 9 onward.
- Do not add rules the sheet does not have. No coins, shops, or tutorial copy. The title is a stage number and always starts at stage 1. The highest stage reached is the only saved progress besides mute.
- A first visit needs no words. The army is already on the road, shooting forward. Sliding moves the whole formation left and right. It advances on its own.
- Start with 1 mameshiba. Gates are blue (`+`, `×`) or red (`−`, `÷`). Each bullet that hits a gate adds 1 to its number. Keep shooting a red gate and it turns blue. At the dog count a good route has when the first red gate arrives, that flip takes about 2.5 seconds. The gate flashes and bounces when the number changes. The first add pairs are blue on both sides and raise the count even if no bullet hits them. One ×2 appears in the first 15 seconds and stays ×2. Red gates appear only after 15 seconds. After 15 seconds a × gate stops at ×2, even if you keep shooting it.
- A drum sometimes sits in front of the better gate. Shooting breaks it. Walking into it removes dogs.
- Bullets fly only across the formation, not the whole road. Formation width stops at one third of the road; extra dogs lengthen the formation backward. Draw at most 80 dogs. Past that, pack them tighter and enlarge the count above the formation.
- Cat hordes come from the front. The late hordes share one fixed size chosen for a large army. They do not grow when the player has more dogs. Each horde has a thin lane. Dodging into it loses about 5% of the dogs. Staying in the gate lane loses about 25%. Only a cat that overlaps the formation takes 1 dog. A good route arrives at the boss with about 440. A route that misses about half the gates loses at least 30% on the thick lane and barely wins stages 1 and 2. Standing still does not lose dogs in the first 15 seconds, then loses. The route that takes every best grown gate and breaks every drum on that path arrives with about 440 dogs before the boss. On stage 3 the late cats are shield cats: frontal shots hit the shield, and contact removes 3 dogs.
- The boss is last. It walks toward the army and reaches it in about 20 seconds. If it is still alive when it arrives, you lose. Boss HP does not grow with the troop. A skilled run leaves about 11 seconds on stage 1, about 8 on stage 2, about 4 on stage 3, and about 2.7 on stage 4. That leftover time gets shorter through stage 4. Stages 5–8 leave about 2.4 seconds, which is not longer than stage 4. Missing about half the gates barely wins stages 1 and 2 and loses from stage 3. The clear screen shows the seconds left before the boss would have arrived. During that fight, tapping the captain icon fires a rocket that takes a visible chunk of the bar. A short clear screen then starts the next stage with 1 dog. One tap retries the same stage after a loss. The stage number is on the HUD. The troop count stays fully on screen, including when the army is on the left edge, above the captain.
- A sliding gate that the formation is not standing on does not add or remove dogs. The first sliding pair is a small blue pair. Later misses skip that gate's dogs.
- Dogs also drop when a drum is not broken before the formation hits it. Threats come from in front of the screen.
- Zero mameshiba is a loss. One tap retries the same stage.
- The captain is the muscle shiba from hirari2 (the same pictures as deep-three): orange fur, cream muzzle, green tank, black shorts. Beans are that picture, smaller. Cats are original. No real athletes or existing characters.
- SFX and music are Web Audio only. No audio files. Create and resume `AudioContext` synchronously on `touchend`/`pointerup` (and again on later taps while it is not running). A silent looping audio element and `navigator.audioSession.type = 'playback'` keep sound on when the iPhone ringer is silent. Schedule music on `currentTime` with a lookahead, not with `setInterval`. Suspend the context while the page is hidden and resume it when visible or on `pageshow`. The march is about 132 BPM. The boss loop gets faster and adds layers as the boss gets closer. Clear and loss each play a short jingle. Mute defaults off and stays off unless the stored value is exactly `1`.
- Mute is stored. The highest stage reached is stored. The title still starts at stage 1.
